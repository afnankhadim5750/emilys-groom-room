the grooming process

The Ritual, Step by Step

New to the Groom Room? Here is how an appointment at Musgrove Road unfolds.

1 · Arrive & Consult

Every appointment begins with a coat consultation. We talk through your dog's coat, skin and temperament, and choose the treatments that suit them best.

2 · Bathe & Pamper

A luxury bath with premium, coat-kind products, followed by the groom or spa ritual you have chosen — all at your dog's pace, one-to-one and cage-free.

3 · The Finishing Flourish

Precise styling, a silk bow and a spritz of cologne — then a dog that prances out the door, ready for compliments all the way home.
